About us

Built by developers, for developers

We're a bunch of Android engineers, hobbyists, and students in Bangalore who meet up every so often to talk Kotlin, Compose, tooling, and whatever's breaking our builds this month. Every session is organized by volunteers from the community, nobody's getting paid, everybody's showing up because they want to.

Sessions are free, informal, and open to anyone curious about Android, from folks shipping their first app to people who've been doing this since Eclair. We meet at partner venues around the city; each event's exact spot and time gets posted on Luma.

Got a talk in you? We want it.

Lightning talk or a full session, first-time speaker or old hand: if you've built, broken, or learned something about Android lately, we want to hear it. Submit through Sessionize and we'll get back to you.
